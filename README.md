### My vision

Entrepreneurship used to feel a bit abstract to me: build something, get lucky, somehow become a founder. CDTM changed that. Being around real founders made me see that startups are not just ideas and luck, but finding a painful problem, talking to users, selling before it feels comfortable, earning trust, and pushing through uncertainty.

As I finish my studies, I want to join an ambitious early-stage startup and learn how great builders actually work from the inside: how they choose what matters, move fast, talk to users, and sell. I also want to keep going deeper technically, because I don’t like using things as magic boxes.

In five years, I want to be much closer to becoming the kind of founder I admire: technical enough to build, commercial enough to sell, and experienced enough to know what I’m doing when it’s my turn.
